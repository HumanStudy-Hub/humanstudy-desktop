# Protocol sample: Believing what you're told

Paper and printed materials: CC BY 4.0, stated on PDF page 11. Original PDF SHA-256: 1d2f8db33a2f14e8b08b549ecbb5b4d88483c53af0790c1ddb6123bd1fe801da. The supplied PDF is unchanged. Participant text is unchanged from the independently reviewed conversion (protocol SHA-256 before app packaging: 47be20b30801cc9d1dc59f1e5b905998ba62a9c65fa3f0b6f8c3974a80f4663d). App packaging adds the schema version and title, and makes the paper path portable; it changes no experimental rule or wording.

Both studies are included, with face-threat/face-boost assignment inside each study. The preview shows seeded example paths, not every possible session. Assignment, option order and navigation assumptions remain labelled derived in the protocol. Consent, button labels and debrief are unavailable and omitted; see meta.omitted_decision. Reaction-time and repeated-click exclusions, RT analyses and regression results are outside the replay's scope. Sample size is the analysed sample, not recruitment.

The independent review and its resolved findings are under review/. Review is not proof of equivalence to human behaviour. No participant-level data is included.
